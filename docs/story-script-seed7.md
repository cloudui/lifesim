# Target script: seed 7, told as one person

A hand-written version of Axel's story (seed 7), to test whether a story about one person with specific moments is compelling before any generator is built. It is a target, not output.

**Note, 2026-11-13:** the "being there" rule added after this was written changed every simulated life, so the seed 7 on the page is now a different life (its story is about Kofi, a childhood friend). This script is kept as the target for tone and for what a beat should show.

Every beat is marked with where it would come from:

- **[log]** the event is already in the simulated life.
- **[detail]** the event is real; the specifics are invented in the telling from the two people's traits and hobbies.
- **[engine]** nothing in the simulation produces this today.

Axel: born 2007, spontaneous, runs, climbs, does pub quizzes. Priya: born 2008, spontaneous, dances.

---

**1. Heron & Magpie Café, San Francisco, 2021. You are 14.**
Your mom sends you in to collect an order. A girl is showing her cousin a dance step between the tables and takes your tray out with her elbow. She buys the replacement with her own money and then drinks half of it. That is Priya.
The chance you were both there that week: 1 in 75,000.
*[log] the meeting, place and odds. [detail] the tray.*

**2. Four years, quickly.**
Three short cuts, a few seconds each, no caption longer than a line:
- Her garage. She has a routine to learn and you are holding the phone. You film the ceiling.
- Your fire escape, the night before a chemistry exam. She quizzes you. You get four out of twenty and she says that's a pass in spirit.
- Walking home from her sixteenth birthday. You talk about who you each like. Neither of you says the obvious thing.
*[log] how often you saw each other before 18. [detail] all three scenes. This is the beat that earns everything after it.*

**3. The table, 2026. You are 19.**
Dad, Mom, and an empty place where your brother Ben sat before he left for Chicago. Priya is in it, most Sundays.
*[log] who is at the table. [detail] Priya in Ben's place; the log would need to confirm she ate with your family.*

**4. Magpie & Kettle Café, 2027.**
Priya brings someone. "This is Tomas. He never plans anything either." She is right; you and Tomas are friends for 22 years.
*[log]*

**5. 2030. You are 23 and moving to New York.**
She helps you pack and leaves wearing your grey hoodie. You say you'll call every week. For a while you do.
*[log] the move, and that calls thinned. [detail] the hoodie.*

**6. Seven years.**
A card, and the first number in the story: between 14 and 23 you saw Priya several hundred times. In the next seven years, not once.
Under it, messages getting further apart: a birthday, a photo of the café, a reply sent two weeks late.
*[log] both counts and the messages.*

**7. 2035. She moves to New York.**
A message: "guess who lives forty minutes from you". You look at it for three days before you answer.
*[log] her move. [detail] the three days.*

**8. 2036. Ben dies. He was 39.**
You have not seen Priya in seven years and she has lived in your city for six months. She is the one who comes. She brings food from a place you have never heard of and does not ask how you are. Speech bubbles for the first time in the story, and they are small.
*[log] Ben's death, and that the two of you met again within the year. [engine] that she came because of it. Today the reunion is a coincidence in a café.*

**9. December 2038.**
Nobody makes it home this year. You and Priya cook in your apartment. It is not good. You do it again the next December, and the one after.
*[log] the December without family. [detail] the dinner. [engine] a couple or a pair of friends having a place and a habit of their own.*

**10. 2040. The fight.**
She says you only turn up when something is wrong. You say that's not fair, and it is a little fair. Three months of nothing.
Then she is at your climbing gym, in rented shoes, six feet off the ground and furious about it.
*[engine] There is no falling-out between these two in the log. Fallings-out exist but are rare, have no reason, and almost never hit the people the story is about.*

**11. 2043. You are 36.**
Tomas's birthday. She pulls you onto the floor; you have been refusing since you were 15. Twenty-two years after the tray.
*[log] getting together, and how long it took. [detail] the dance floor.*

**12. The table, December 2043, in Chicago.**
Priya's first time at the table as your partner. Your mom has been setting a place for her since she was 14.
*[log]*

**13. Without her.**
The screen empties to you and Priya. Then Tomas fades in beside her. Then Ezra, beside Tomas. Then nine more, one at a time, each joined by a line to the person who brought them. Eleven of the people in your life are there because a girl knocked over a tray.
*[log] who led to whom. This is the consequence beat: it needs no new simulation, only a scene.*

**14. 2049. Back to San Francisco, together.**
Tomas helps load the van. It is the last time you see him. Neither of you knows.
*[log] the move and the last meeting.*

**15. The table, December 2056.**
Dad's chair went empty in 2050 and Mom's this year. The table you grew up at is gone. The one you sit at has Priya across it.
*[log]*

**16. The Golden Lantern, 2065. You are 58.**
Every couple of months, the same table by the window. Forty-four years since the café. 1 in 75,000.
Then: "There were 402 others." and the door into the rest of the life.
*[log] the standing night out, the totals.*

## What this script needed that does not exist

1. **Childhood moments** (beat 2). The friendship predates the story, so the earning has to be invented or the story has to start earlier than 18.
2. **Hard stretches that pull people in** (beat 8). A death or a bad year should change who shows up, and showing up should build depth fast.
3. **Fights with a reason, between people who matter** (beat 10).
4. **A place and a habit for a pair** (beats 9 and 16), including a couple.
5. **A detail library** (the tray, the hoodie, the rented shoes): invented specifics chosen from traits and hobbies, kept consistent so the hoodie can come back.

Beats 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11 to 16 need no engine work, only better telling and new scenes.
